## 4. UNITS, CURRENCIES AND CONVENTIONS (MANDATORY CORRECTNESS)

**United Kingdom**
- Distance: miles; speed: mph.
- Fuel economy: MPG (UK imperial), based on 1 imperial gallon = 4.54609 L.
- Fuel is sold **in litres**, priced in **pence per litre (p/l)** — a genuine UK-specific oddity worth explaining: the pump uses litres while the economy figure uses MPG.
- Currency: GBP (£).
- Driving charges: VED (road tax), MOT, ULEZ/LEZ, Congestion Charge, Dartford Crossing, M6 Toll.

**United States**
- Distance: miles; speed: mph.
- Fuel economy: MPG (US), based on 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L.
- Fuel is sold **in US gallons**, priced in **USD per gallon**, typically with octane grades (87/89/93) and diesel.
- Currency: USD ($).
- Fuel economy ratings come from EPA; EV efficiency commonly in **kWh/100 mi**, **mi/kWh**, or **MPGe**.
- Electricity commonly priced in **cents per kWh**.
- Tolls: state turnpikes, bridges, tunnels, transponders (E-ZPass, SunPass, etc.).
- No motorway vignette anywhere in the US.

**Continental Europe**
- Distance: kilometres; fuel economy: litres per 100 km; volume in litres; EUR or local currency; vignettes in AT/CH/SI and others.

**Never confuse UK MPG with US MPG.** The two differ by a factor of ≈1.20095. A page that does not state which one it means is wrong.

Support appropriate local units, currencies, vehicle terminology, number formats, decimal/thousand separators, date formats, and language conventions. Where a user is likely to switch between systems (UK↔EU, US↔metric, tourist contexts), offer an explicit toggle — never a silent assumption.

## 30. CALCULATION ACCURACY

Write and test the formula before building the UI. Use a central formula library and independent unit tests. At minimum verify:

- **L/100 km → MPG:** MPG(UK) = 282.480936 ÷ (L/100 km); MPG(US) = 235.214583 ÷ (L/100 km). Valid for positive values; handle 0 explicitly (infinite MPG, not an error).
- **kW → horsepower:** PS = kW × 1.359621617; hp = kW × 1.34102209. Document unit definitions and verify constants.
- **Fuel cost:** Litres = Distance(km) × Consumption(L/100 km) ÷ 100; Cost = Litres × price per litre. US path: Gallons = Distance(mi) ÷ MPG(US); Cost = Gallons × price per gallon.
- **EV charging cost:** Cost = Energy purchased(kWh) × price per kWh; when deriving energy from a desired battery-energy increase, account explicitly for charging-efficiency.
- **Petrol/gas vs EV:** both sides computed on the same distance basis; petrol cost per 100 km = consumption × fuel price; EV cost per 100 km = EV consumption × electricity price.
- **Supporting constants to verify and document:** 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L; 1 imperial gallon = 4.54609 L; 1 mile = 1.609344 km; UK/US MPG ratio ≈ 1.20095; EPA MPGe basis (33.7 kWh per gallon of gasoline equivalent — verify against the current EPA source); pence per litre ↔ £ per litre; cents per kWh ↔ $ per kWh.

Also: verify each formula independently; test zero, negative, missing, very large, and decimal inputs; test boundaries; avoid division-by-zero; use appropriate precision and sensible rounding; distinguish rounding from the underlying calculation; document assumptions; do not confuse estimates with guaranteed costs; do not invent formulas for road, tax, or toll regulations.

For changing tolls, vignettes, taxes, and regulations, use the official authority's documented rules and prices. Mark unverifiable formulas as unverified. Maintain a verification log: formula, source, test inputs, expected output, actual output, verification date.

**Additional accuracy rule for this version:** every conversion output must be labelled with the unit system used, and every page must state which country's convention its defaults follow.
